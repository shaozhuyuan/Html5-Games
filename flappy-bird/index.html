About Flappy Coder

Flappy Coder is a retro-style, programmer-themed arcade game based on the addictive flappy mechanics. Guide the flying website icon safely through columns of bugs, compilation hazards, and merge conflicts.

This mini-game is optimized for web browser play with intuitive spacebar/touch controls and adjusted difficulty curves. Test your reflexes, avoid server crashes, and see how long you can keep the production service active!
